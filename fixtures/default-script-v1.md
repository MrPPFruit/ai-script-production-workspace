## 1. ACT I, SCENE I — On a ship at sea

### English source excerpt (public domain)

SCENE I. On a ship at sea: a tempestuous noise of thunder and lightning heard.

Enter a Master and a Boatswain

MASTER: Boatswain!

BOATSWAIN: Here, master: what cheer?

MASTER: Good, speak to the mariners: fall to't, yarely, or we run ourselves aground: bestir, bestir.

Exit. Enter Mariners

BOATSWAIN: Heigh, my hearts! cheerly, cheerly, my hearts! yare, yare! Take in the topsail. Tend to the master's whistle. Blow, till thou burst thy wind, if room enough!

## 2. ACT I, SCENE II — Before Prospero's cell

### English source excerpt (public domain)

MIRANDA sleeps.

PROSPERO: Come away, servant, come. I am ready now. Approach, my Ariel, come.

Enter ARIEL

ARIEL: All hail, great master! grave sir, hail! I come to answer thy best pleasure; be't to fly, to swim, to dive into the fire, to ride on the curl'd clouds, to thy strong bidding task Ariel and all his quality.

PROSPERO: Hast thou, spirit, perform'd to point the tempest that I bade thee?

ARIEL: To every article. I boarded the king's ship; now on the beak, now in the waist, the deck, in every cabin, I flamed amazement: sometime I'ld divide, and burn in many places; on the topmast, the yards and bowsprit, would I flame distinctly, then meet and join. Jove's lightnings, the precursors o' the dreadful thunder-claps, more momentary and sight-outrunning were not; the fire and cracks of sulphurous roaring the most mighty Neptune seem to besiege and make his bold waves tremble, yea, his dread trident shake.

### 简体中文对照（本项目自行翻译／演示用途）

米兰达睡去。

普洛斯彼罗：出来吧，仆人，出来。我已经准备好了。靠近些，我的爱丽儿，来吧。

爱丽儿上。

爱丽儿：向伟大的主人致敬！尊贵的先生，向您致敬！我来听候您的一切吩咐：无论飞翔、游水、潜入火中，还是乘着卷曲的云朵，只要您一声令下，爱丽儿和他的同伴都愿效劳。

普洛斯彼罗：精灵，我命你制造的暴风雨，可曾丝毫不差地完成？

爱丽儿：每一项都完成了。我登上国王的船，一会儿在船首，一会儿在船腰、甲板和每一间船舱燃起令人惊骇的火焰。我有时分散，在多处燃烧；在主桅、横桁和斜桅上各自腾起，再汇成一体。哪怕朱庇特的闪电——那可怕雷霆的先声——也没有这般转瞬即逝、快过视线。火焰与硫磺般的轰鸣仿佛围攻强大的海神，使他的巨浪战栗，连可怖的三叉戟也为之震动。

## 3. ACT III, SCENE III — Another part of the island

### English source excerpt (public domain)

ALONSO: I will stand to and feed, although my last: no matter, since I feel the best is past. Brother, my lord the duke, stand to and do as we.

Thunder and lightning. Enter ARIEL, like a harpy; claps his wings upon the table; and, with a quaint device, the banquet vanishes.

ARIEL: You are three men of sin, whom Destiny, that hath to instrument this lower world and what is in't, the never-surfeited sea hath caused to belch up you; and on this island where man doth not inhabit; you 'mongst men being most unfit to live. I have made you mad; and even with such-like valour men hang and drown their proper selves.

ALONSO, SEBASTIAN & c. draw their swords.

ARIEL: You fools! I and my fellows are ministers of Fate: the elements, of whom your swords are temper'd, may as well wound the loud winds, or with bemock'd-at stabs kill the still-closing waters, as diminish one dowle that's in my plume: my fellow-ministers are like invulnerable.

He vanishes in thunder; then, to soft music enter the Shapes again, and dance, with mocks and mows, and carrying out the table.

### 简体中文对照（本项目自行翻译／演示用途）

阿隆索：我就上前进食吧，即便这是最后一餐也无妨，因为我觉得最好的时光已经过去。弟弟，还有公爵大人，都上前来，和我们一起吧。

雷电交作。爱丽儿化作鹰身女妖上场；他用翅膀拍击桌面，借一套巧妙机关使宴席消失。

爱丽儿：你们三个罪人，命运借永不餍足的大海支配尘世万物，又让海把你们吐上这座无人居住的岛。你们在人类之中，最不配活着。我已经使你们发狂；人正会凭着这种疯狂的勇气，把自己吊死或溺死。

阿隆索、塞巴斯蒂安等人拔剑。

爱丽儿：蠢人！我和同伴都是命运的使者。铸成你们宝剑的元素，若想伤我一根羽毛，就如同想刺伤呼啸的风，或用受嘲弄的剑击杀不断合拢的水一样徒劳；我的同伴也同样刀枪不入。

他在雷声中消失；随后柔和的音乐响起，异形表演者再次上场，一边嘲弄地舞蹈，一边把桌子搬走。
